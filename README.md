**AI-powered Sports, Fitness, Nutrition & Professional Discovery Assistant**

AthletiQ AI combines conversational AI with real-world location-aware discovery to provide personalized sports and fitness guidance while helping users discover nearby gyms, academies, trainers, nutritionists, and other professionals.

**✨ Key Features**
- 🤖 Conversational AI sports & fitness assistant
- 🧠 Context-aware responses based on athlete preferences
- 🥗 Personalized nutrition and meal guidance

- 🗺️ Interactive Google Maps integration
- 🏋️ Nearby gyms, academies, trainers & nutritionists
