## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏃 AthletiQ AI

**AI-Powered Sports, Fitness, Nutrition & Professional Discovery Assistant**

AthletiQ AI is a full-stack AI application designed to bring sports guidance, fitness education,
nutrition assistance, and location-aware professional discovery into one intelligent platform.

It combines conversational AI with real-world location data, allowing users to receive personalized
guidance while discovering nearby gyms, sports academies, trainers, nutritionists, and other
fitness professionals.

#### ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 Conversational AI assistant for sports, fitness & nutrition
- 🧠 Context-aware responses based on athlete preferences
- 🏃 Support for multiple sports and fitness activities
- 🥗 Personalized nutrition and meal guidance
- 📍 Location-aware facility and professional discovery
- 🗺️ Interactive Google Maps integration
- 🏋️ Discover nearby gyms, academies, trainers & nutritionists
- ⚙️ Customizable sport, experience level, fitness goal & dietary preferences
- 🔎 Google Places powered real-world search

#### 🛠️ Tech Stack

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Node.js` `Express.js`  
`Google Gemini` `Google Maps Platform` `Google Places API` `Google Cloud`
